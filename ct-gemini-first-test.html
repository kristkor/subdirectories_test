Our story

Halden & Rowe started in 2014 in a rented garage on the edge of Porto, with two second-hand sewing machines and a stubborn idea: that a bag should be repaired, not replaced. Every piece is still cut by hand from full-grain hides that we buy from a single family tannery in Alcanena, about two hours south of the workshop. We never use corrected or split leather, which is why the surface of each bag looks slightly different and why it darkens and softens as you use it. If a seam ever comes loose, send the bag back to us and we will repair it for free, for as long as you own it.

Our workshop is open to visitors on the first Saturday of every month between 10 am and 4 pm. You can watch the team cut and edge the leather, try on pieces from the current collection and see the archive of prototypes that never made it into the shop. The visit is free, but places are limited to twelve people, so please book your spot online at least a week in advance. Children are welcome, dogs too, as long as they stay away from the cutting table. Parking is available in front of the building, and the metro stop Bolhão is a ten-minute walk away.

Shipping inside Portugal takes one to two business days and is free on every order. Orders to the rest of the European Union arrive within three to five business days; delivery is free above €120 and costs €9.50 below that. For the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Norway we ship with prepaid duties, so the price you see at checkout is the price you pay. You can return any unused item within 30 days for a full refund. Monograms, custom sizes and repaired items cannot be returned, but we are always happy to find a solution with you.

Frequently asked questions

How do I care for my bag?

Wipe it with a dry cloth and apply a thin layer of leather balm twice a year. Keep it away from radiators and direct sunlight.

Can I change my order after it has been placed?

Yes, within one hour of purchase. After that, please write to us and we will do our best before the parcel leaves the workshop.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Every order ships in a cotton dust bag. Gift wrapping with a handwritten card costs €4 and can be added in the cart.

Is the leather waterproof?

No. Light rain is fine, but a heavy shower can leave marks. Let the bag dry naturally and never use a hairdryer.
