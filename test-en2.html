How we make it

Halden Soap Kitchen began in 2018 on a two-ring cooker in a tenement flat in Leith, after one of us spent a whole winter trying to find a soap that did not make her hands crack. Today we work in an old net loft overlooking the Water of Leith, but we still make every batch the slow way: oils and lye are mixed by hand, poured into wooden moulds, cut the next morning and left to cure on open racks for at least four weeks. We never add palm oil, synthetic colours or microplastics, and every bar is wrapped in recycled paper printed with vegetable ink. If a bar ever cracks or sweats before you have used it, send us a photo and we will post you a new one, no questions asked.

On the second Wednesday of every month we open the kitchen in the evening for a soap-making class. You will make four bars of your own with a scent of your choosing, learn why soap needs time to cure, and take home a recipe card so you can try it again in your own kitchen. Gloves, goggles and aprons are provided, and so is a pot of tea. The class costs £45 and lasts about three hours, but there are only eight places, so please book your place online at least a week in advance. Your bars will be ready to collect four weeks later, or we can post them to you for £3.

Orders to mainland UK addresses usually arrive within three working days, and delivery is free when you spend £35 or more; below that it costs £3.95. We ship to the Highlands and Islands at the same price, although it can take a day longer. Everything is packed in cardboard and paper tape, so the whole parcel can go into your recycling. Unopened products can be returned within 14 days for a full refund. For hygiene reasons we cannot take back anything that has been opened, but if something has arrived damaged, tell us within a week and we will replace it.

Questions we get asked a lot

Is your soap suitable for sensitive skin?

Our unscented oat bar was made for exactly that. If you have eczema or a known allergy, have a look at the full ingredient list on each product page before you order.

How long does a bar last?

Used every day and kept dry between washes, one bar lasts about six weeks. A draining soap dish makes more difference than you would think.

Do you sell to shops and hotels?

Yes, from 40 bars per order, with your own label if you like. Send us a short message and we will reply with a price list within two working days.

Can I refill my own bottles?

Bring any clean bottle to our refill station on Saturdays between 10:00 and 16:00 and pay by weight. Liquid soap and shampoo are 2.4 pence per gram.
