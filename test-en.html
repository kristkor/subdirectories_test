Our story

Spokesworth Cycle Works started in 2014 in a former piano warehouse just off Stokes Croft, with two second-hand workstands, a borrowed truing jig and a rather stubborn idea: a good bicycle should outlive at least three owners. Every repair is still done by hand, and every wheel we sell is laced, tensioned and trued on our own bench instead of arriving pre-built in a box. We never fit a part we would not ride on ourselves, which is why we sometimes talk customers out of an expensive upgrade rather than selling it to them. If anything we have fitted fails within a year, bring the bike back and we will put it right free of charge, whoever turns out to be at fault.

On the last Thursday of every month we keep the doors open until nine for our evening maintenance club. You can bring your own bike, borrow a stand and a set of tools, and learn how to fix a puncture, adjust a rear derailleur or swap worn brake pads while one of our mechanics keeps an eye on you. The evening costs £8, tea and biscuits included, but there is room for only twelve bikes, so please reserve a stand online a few days ahead. Children over ten are welcome when they come with an adult. Temple Meads station is a fifteen-minute walk away, and there is covered cycle parking right outside the door, which seems only fair.

Orders to mainland UK addresses arrive in two to three working days, and delivery is free on anything over £60; below that it costs £4.95. Parcels to the Scottish Highlands, Northern Ireland and the islands can take an extra day or two. We also ship to the European Union and Norway with duties prepaid, so the price you see at checkout is the final amount you pay. Unused items can be sent back within 30 days for a full refund, and the return label is on us. Custom wheel builds and parts cut to length cannot be returned, but if something is not right, give us a call and we will sort it out together.

Frequently asked questions

How often should my bike be serviced?

For a commuter who rides most days, every six months is about right. If you ride through a Bristol winter, a quick check in March will save you money later on.

Can I wait while you fix a puncture?

Yes, walk-in punctures usually take about twenty minutes. Bigger jobs need a booking, and most bikes are ready the next working day.

Do you work on electric bikes?

We service the bike itself, including brakes, gears and wheels. Motor and battery faults go back to the manufacturer, and we are happy to arrange that for you.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes, from £20 upwards. They stay valid for two years and can be spent on repairs, parts or a place at the maintenance club.
