Switcher theme demo pages

One made-up site per theme, each built to show that theme at its best. The switcher sits in the bottom-right corner of every page.

  • GlassHalde, a mountain hut, over a painted dusk landscape
  • NeonPulse Room, a late-night club
  • BubbleGlaze & Co., a stoneware shop with a cart and a chat button
  • AutoLedgerly, a SaaS page with its own dark-mode switch

Taking the screenshots

  1. The switcher's theme comes from the domain's widget settings, so every page on this domain shows the same theme at a time.
  2. In the dashboard, pick the theme for this domain and save, then open its page here and reload.
  3. On the Auto page, use the Dark mode button to capture the light and the dark look.
