Bakery test page

Our bakery opens every morning at seven o'clock and closes in the early evening. We bake fresh sourdough bread, butter croissants and cinnamon rolls before the doors open. Every recipe uses flour from a small mill in the next valley. The coffee comes from a local family roaster who visits us every week. Children love the chocolate muffins that we make on Saturdays. On Sundays the shop stays closed so that the team can rest. In summer we also sell homemade lemonade and fruit tarts on the terrace. Groups can book the back room for birthdays and small meetings.
