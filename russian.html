Context and ambiguity test

The word “light” can describe colour, weight or illumination. In this collection, a light bag may be pale in colour, easy to carry, or both. Likewise, “statement piece” does not mean a written statement, and a “drop” may refer to a new product release rather than something falling.

“Complimentary” can mean free of charge, while “complementary” describes things that work well together. A “case” may be a container, a situation, or a grammatical category. Good translation depends on context, not on replacing one word with another.
