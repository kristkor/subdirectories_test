How rental works

When you reserve online, tell us your height, weight, shoe size and how confident you feel on the slopes. Our technicians use that information to prepare and adjust your equipment the evening before your arrival, so all you need to do at the counter is try the boots on and sign the rental form. Every ski is waxed and the edges are sharpened after each rental, and if anything feels wrong during the day you can swap it at any of our shops without paying extra. You can also extend your rental from your phone until 8 pm the day before it ends.

Our ski school runs group lessons for beginners every morning at 9:30 and private lessons all day by appointment. Groups have at most six skiers and are sorted by age and level, so a nervous adult will never end up in a class of fearless teenagers. Children under seven start in the snow garden next to the shop, where the slope is gentle and the instructors use games to teach balance and stopping. For off-piste days we work with certified mountain guides, and avalanche equipment is included in every guided tour. Book a lesson at least 24 hours ahead in high season.

If the lifts close because of wind or heavy snowfall, we refund the full rental price for that day automatically, without you having to ask. Cancellations made more than 48 hours before the start date are free; after that we keep 20 percent of the price. Damage from normal use, such as small scratches on the base, is always covered. For broken skis or lost poles we charge a fixed fee of NOK 500, unless you added our damage waiver for NOK 40 per day at checkout.

Questions before your trip

Can I bring my own boots?

Yes. Bring them when you pick up your skis and we will adjust the bindings to your boots for free.

Do you store our equipment overnight?

Every shop has heated lockers. Leave your skis and boots there after the last run and they will be dry the next morning.

What happens if my child is too small for the helmet?

We stock helmets from 48 cm head size. Bring your child to the shop and we will find one that fits before you pay.

Is the snow report updated every day?

The report is updated at 7 am and again at noon, with snow depth, open runs and the avalanche danger level.
